**The pocket knife for your Mac.** The first complete version: one small menu bar app that replaces Hammerspoon, MTMR, noTunes, Maccy and Lightshot. Every part is a module you can turn on or off.

### What's inside

- **Touch Bar**: brightness, keyboard light, volume, music, weather, CPU temperature, battery, speed test and time. State icons; choose and reorder the buttons.
- **Music**: play/pause, next and previous for the player you choose. Headphone clicks go to that player, and Apple Music no longer opens by itself.
- **Clipboard history** with pinned items, search and ⌘1–⌘9.
- **Screenshot editor**: frozen or live, area or window; arrows, text, highlighter, shapes, pen and pixelate; copy or save.
- **Mouse jiggler** with interval, **microphone mute**, **lock screen**, **speed test** (`networkQuality`), **alerts** and status dots in the menu bar.
- **Settings**: one System Settings style sidebar (General, Permissions, Shortcuts, every module, About).
- **A little extra**: hold the jiggler button for 3 seconds and slice fruit on the Touch Bar (about 4 % CPU).

### Install

1. Download **Penknife-1.0.zip** below, unzip it and move `Penknife.app` to `/Applications`.
2. Open it. Penknife is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

Requires macOS 26 or later. The Touch Bar module needs a MacBook Pro with a Touch Bar.
